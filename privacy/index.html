Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Mote is a minimal, local-first Markdown note app. This policy explains what data Mote stores, what may leave your device, and how the app is designed to protect your notes.

Notes and app data

Mote does not require an account. Your notes, groups, and app settings are stored locally in your browser or installed app using browser storage such as IndexedDB.

Mote does not send the contents of your notes to a Mote server by default.

Backups, imports, and exports

Importing Markdown files or restoring a backup is performed on your device. Exported Markdown, text, PDF, or backup files are created at your request and are saved to a location you choose.

External links and remote content

Notes may contain links or images hosted by third parties. Opening an external link or rendering remote content may cause your browser or device to connect to that third party. Their own privacy policies apply to those connections.

Website and hosting data

Mote is delivered through web hosting infrastructure. Like most web services, hosting and network providers may process technical request information such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, and diagnostic or security data as part of operating and protecting the service.

Analytics and advertising

Mote does not include advertising and does not use note content for advertising. Mote does not intentionally send note content to analytics services.

Data sharing and sale

Mote does not sell your notes or personal data. Mote does not provide your note content to third parties except when an action you take causes a connection to a third-party service, such as opening a link or loading a remote image.

Data deletion

Because Mote is local-first, you control the data stored on your device. You can delete notes in the app, permanently remove items from Trash, or clear the browser or app storage. Clearing local storage may permanently remove data that has not been exported or backed up.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Mote's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Questions?

You can contact the project through GitHub Issues.
